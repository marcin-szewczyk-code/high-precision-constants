# The Constant π

The mathematical constant **π** represents the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter.

Unlike the constant e, π does not admit a simple rapidly convergent factorial series. Several infinite series and algorithms can be used for its computation.

## Planned Methods

- Gregory–Leibniz series  
- Ramanujan series  
- Chudnovsky series  

These methods differ significantly in convergence speed and computational efficiency.

The goal of this section is to explore and compare these approaches from a numerical perspective.
